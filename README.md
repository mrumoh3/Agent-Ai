🎯 Task Title: “Kickstarting AgentVault’s First 50 Users”
Scenario:

You’ve just joined AgentVault as a Business Development Intern. Our product—a smart, AI-powered crypto trading assistant—is at MVP stage. Your job is to help us find, engage, and activate early users and partners.

🧪 Your Task:

Please answer the following in a Google Doc, Notion page, or PDF (1–2 pages max):

1. Identify 2 Target User Segments

Who should we target first? Pick 2 specific user types (e.g., crypto traders, DAO treasurers, DeFi founders, student investors, etc.).
👉 For each, answer:

    Why them?

    What pain point does AgentVault solve for them?

    Where would you find them online/offline?

2. Outreach Example

Write a sample cold message/email you would send to one of those user types to pitch AgentVault.
👉 Bonus if you tailor it to Discord, Twitter, or LinkedIn.
3. 2–3 GTM Ideas or Campaigns

Share 2–3 creative, low-cost ideas you’d test to get our first 50 users or partners.
Examples:

    “Launch a crypto meme contest”

    “Target university blockchain clubs via Discord and pitch an early access beta”

No need for polish—we want to see how you think and take initiative.

💡 What We’re Evaluating:

    Hustle, creativity, and clarity

    Communication style
